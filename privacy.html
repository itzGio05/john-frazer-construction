Privacy

This website is for John Frazer Construction, a construction company in Ireland. It is a simple information page and is built to collect as little about you as possible.

  • No forms. The site does not ask you for your name, email or any other details.
  • No analytics or advertising. We do not track your visit or build a profile of you.
  • No third-party content loads on the page. Fonts, images and video are served from this site itself.
  • Hosting logs. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may record technical details of requests, such as your IP address, to run and secure the service. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.
  • Links you choose to follow. Tapping a phone number starts a call with your own phone app. Map and review links open Google Maps, which has its own privacy policy.

If you phone us, we use what you tell us only to respond to your enquiry.

Questions about your data? Call 087 236 7537.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies and uses no similar tracking technology, so there is no cookie banner to accept or reject.

Terms of use

The information on this site is general and is not a quotation, an offer or a contract. Any building work is agreed directly with John Frazer Construction.

The rating, review count, opening hours and customer reviews shown are taken from the public Google listing for John Frazer Construction and are quoted as they appear there. The photographs are from the same listing.

We aim to keep this page accurate, but details can change. Please phone to confirm anything important.
